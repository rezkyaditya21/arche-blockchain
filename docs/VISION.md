# ARCHE Vision

## Why ARCHE Exists

Artificial intelligence is reshaping the world — but access to it remains centralized.
The most capable AI models are controlled by a handful of large corporations.
There is no standard way to prove that a model actually ran the computation it claimed to run.
Payments for AI services must go through platforms that can block, monitor, or cut access at any time.

ARCHE is built to change that.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Computational Sovereignty
Anyone should be able to sell or buy AI computation without permission from a third party.
A worker anywhere in the world can register, offer their compute capability, and get paid directly.

### 2. Verification, Not Trust
AI results cannot simply be trusted — they must be verifiable.
ARCHE builds 5 levels of verification from the simplest (hash comparison)
to the strongest (zero-knowledge proof — currently in research).

### 3. Machine Economy
AI agents must be able to transact directly with each other without human intervention.
ARC is the shared economic language for all machines in the ARCHE ecosystem.

### 4. Fair Supply
Total ARC supply is 50,000,000 — cannot be increased, cannot be changed.
Distribution happens through open mining available to anyone.
No pre-mine, no hidden allocations.

### 5. Security Above All
New features will not compromise consensus security.
Every change must pass audit and tests before entering production.
Experimental modules remain isolated from the core blockchain.

---

## What ARCHE Is Not

- **Not a cloud AI platform** — ARCHE does not run AI models itself
- **Not a general-purpose smart contract platform** — no arbitrary code execution
- **Not a stablecoin or token on another chain** — ARCHE is its own blockchain
- **Not a copy-paste project** — built from scratch, not forked from any other project

---

## The Path Forward

**Short term:** A public network accessible to anyone.

**Medium term:** A decentralized AI worker ecosystem — anyone can offer compute and get paid in ARC.

**Long term:** Economic infrastructure for autonomous machines — AI agents that can work, transact, and collaborate independently using ARC as their medium of exchange.
